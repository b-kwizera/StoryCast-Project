The Bridge Choir

  • Audio · 1 min
  • Captions
  • Transcript

A storm, a hummed tune, and eight years later a neighbourhood still singing under a railway bridge.

Transcript

Download transcript (.txt)

  1. Every Thursday evening, beneath the old railway bridge, thirty voices begin to warm up.

  2. The choir started eight years ago, when a rainstorm forced a few neighbours to shelter in the same spot.

  3. Someone hummed to pass the time. Someone else joined in. By the end of the storm, they had a harmony.

  4. Now the choir has grandparents, students, bus drivers, and one very serious ten year old tenor.

  5. The bridge gives their sound a long, warm echo that no concert hall could copy.

  6. They never perform for money. They sing so the neighbourhood remembers it can still make something together.

  7. If you walk by on a Thursday, stop and listen. They will always save you a place.
