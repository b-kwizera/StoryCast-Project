Before the Market Wakes

  • Video · 1 min
  • Captions
  • Transcript

Amina has opened the same fruit stall for twenty-two years. This is what the hours before sunrise sound like.

Transcript

Download transcript (.txt)

  1. It is four in the morning, and the market is still dark.

  2. Amina unlocks her stall and lifts the first crate of mangoes onto the table.

  3. She has sold fruit here for twenty-two years, ever since her mother handed her the key.

  4. By five, the other vendors arrive, and the whole lane fills with quiet greetings.

  5. Nobody rushes. Each person knows exactly where to place a crate, a scale, a basket.

  6. When the sun finally reaches the tin roofs, the first customers are already waiting.

  7. Amina says the market is not really about selling. It is about being seen, every single day.

  8. And as the light grows, so does the noise, the laughter, and the smell of ripe mango.
