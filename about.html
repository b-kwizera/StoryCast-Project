About StoryCast

StoryCast exists to share ordinary people's stories in a way that anyone can enjoy, whether they listen, watch, read, or use assistive technology.

Our mission

Too many media sites treat captions and transcripts as an afterthought. We build them in from the first line of HTML. A story is not finished until it can be heard, seen, and read.

Accessibility features

  • Captions and transcripts

    Every audio and video story has WebVTT captions and a transcript on the page. Click a timestamp to jump to that moment.

  • Colour contrast

    Text and controls meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Meaning is never carried by colour alone.

  • Keyboard and focus

    A skip link, a logical tab order, and a high-visibility focus ring on every link and button.

  • Screen readers

    Landmarks, one h1 per page, labelled regions, and aria-current for the current page and active transcript line.

  • Flexible layout

    The layout adapts from phone to desktop and survives zooming to 200%. Motion is reduced if your system asks for it.

  • Plain language

    Short sentences and clear headings, so stories and instructions are easy to follow.

How to engage with StoryCast

  1. Pick a story from the home page.
  2. Choose how to take it in: play the audio or video, turn captions on or off in the player, or just read the transcript.
  3. Jump around. Use the timestamp buttons in the transcript to replay any line.
  4. Take the text with you. Each transcript can be downloaded as a plain-text file.

Known limitations

The narration in these demo stories is synthetic speech and the video uses abstract visuals, so there are no on-screen events to describe. A story with real footage would also need audio description. If something on the site blocks you, tell us and we will fix it.
